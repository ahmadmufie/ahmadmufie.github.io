Thesis: making post-quantum TLS lighter

ML-DSA certificates are far larger than ECDSA ones, so a post-quantum TLS 1.3 handshake sends much more data. My thesis designs an adaptive TLS 1.3 capacity profile and tests two ways to cut that overhead: session resumption and certificate chain pruning. All measurements were taken with OpenSSL 3.5.5.

  • 87.00%less handshake traffic with session resumption on ML-DSA-87 (ECDSA-P256: 30.45%)
  • 47.60%less traffic with a leaf-only certificate chain on ML-DSA-87
  • ~8.4 KBresumed handshake size, nearly identical for every algorithm tested
